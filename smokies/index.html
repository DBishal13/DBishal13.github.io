What's open Nov 25–29

Kuwohi Road: open through Nov 29The 2026 season runs 4/1–11/29, so your whole trip fits. Snow or ice can still close it any day, so don't save it for Sunday.
Roaring Fork: open through Nov 29Grotto Falls and the motor nature trail drive are both on this road.
Laurel Falls: closedRehab work is delayed into 2027. Skip it.
Bullhead Trail: open dailyThe Mon–Thu rehab closures end Nov 19.
Parking tag neededYou need one for any stop over 15 min: $5/day or $15/week. Federal passes don't cover it.
Also ends Nov 29Little Greenbrier Rd closes after Nov 29. Parson Branch and Rich Mountain roads already closed on Nov 9.
Weather reality check: Kuwohi is often 15–20°F colder than Gatlinburg, and Newfound Gap Road can close for snow on short notice. Check NPS conditions each morning.
